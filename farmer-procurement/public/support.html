Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register on the platform?

Click on 'Farmer Login' in the top menu and select 'New Registration'. Fill in your mobile number, verify it using OTP, enter your Aadhaar reference number and address, and set a password.

Can I book multiple slots?

Yes. Based on your registered land area and allowed crop produce limit, you can book slots across different dates/times until your total allowed quantity limit is reached.

What if I arrive late to my scheduled slot?

Your booking remains valid for the selected date, but your queue position might be reassigned to minimize center congestion. It is highly recommended to arrive 10 minutes before your slot.

How is the final crop payment calculated?

Payments are calculated using the actual weighed accepted quantity at the center (not the expected quantity booked online) times the state-approved minimum support price (MSP).
